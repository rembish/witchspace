WITCHSPACE

A free reimplementation of Elite Plus (1991), the PC version of Elite.

Ready

The game's files are in this browser from an earlier visit.

For the title, the Elite theme (Aidan Bell's, arranged by C. Abbott) can play instead of the game's own music: add b8060000.mp3 from Ian Bell's Elite archive.

The game's files

Witchspace contains no part of the original game. It needs three files from Elite Plus (ELITE.EXE, ELITE.GRF, ADBLUE.MID, about 350 KB).

It can download them for you from the copy on Ian Bell's Elite pages (Elite's co-author's), through its mirror at the Internet Archive. They then stay in your browser's storage on this computer and are never sent to us or anyone else. Elite Plus is still under copyright (© 1991 Chris Sawyer, Realtime Software, Bell & Braben): whether to use that copy is your decision.

Or use your own copy: choose the three files from your Elite Plus folder, or a zip from Ian Bell's Elite archive as it is (b1022000.zip, the game as sold).

Keys

↑ ↓ pitch, ← → roll, . faster, , slower, Space fire. Commands are the icons along the bottom: press 1–9, 0, -, = (or F1–F12) for the icon in that place. Esc: the menu (options, keys, save, load).

The original's manual explains the rest: trading, combat, equipment, missions.
